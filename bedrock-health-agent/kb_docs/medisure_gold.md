# MediSure — Gold Complete Hospital & Extras

> FICTIONAL PRODUCT — for demo only. Not a real health insurance policy.

- **Fund:** MediSure
- **Product name:** Gold Complete Hospital & Extras
- **Tier:** Gold
- **Cover type:** Comprehensive hospital + comprehensive extras
- **Monthly premium (single, base):** $310
- **State availability:** NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, ACT, TAS
- **Excess:** $250 per admission

## Hospital cover (what's included)
All Gold-tier clinical categories (full list), including:
- Pregnancy and birth
- Joint reconstruction and joint replacement
- Cataracts
- Weight loss surgery
- Dialysis for chronic kidney failure
- Psychiatric services (comprehensive, not restricted)

## Extras cover (annual limits)
- General & major dental: up to $1,500/yr
- Optical: up to $400/yr
- Physiotherapy, chiro, remedial massage: up to $700/yr combined
- Psychology: up to $600/yr

## Waiting periods
- 2 months: general
- 12 months: pre-existing conditions, pregnancy and birth
- 12 months: major dental
- 2–6 months: most extras

## Who it suits
Families and members who want the most comprehensive cover available, including pregnancy and comprehensive psychiatric cover, and are less price-sensitive.
