# SunWell — Silver Active Hospital & Extras

> FICTIONAL PRODUCT — for demo only. Not a real health insurance policy.

- **Fund:** SunWell
- **Product name:** Silver Active Hospital & Extras
- **Tier:** Silver (Plus)
- **Cover type:** Hospital + strong everyday extras
- **Monthly premium (single, base):** $185
- **State availability:** NSW, VIC, QLD, WA
- **Excess:** $500 per admission

## Hospital cover (what's included)
All Silver-tier clinical categories, including:
- Joint reconstruction
- Cataracts
- Hernia and appendix
- Rehabilitation, psychiatric (restricted)

## Extras cover (annual limits) — strong for everyday use
- General dental: up to $800/yr
- Optical: up to $300/yr
- Physiotherapy: up to $500/yr
- Remedial massage: up to $250/yr

## Not covered
- Pregnancy and birth (Gold)
- Major dental / orthodontics
- Comprehensive psychiatric (restricted only)

## Waiting periods
- 2 months: general hospital and extras
- 6 months: major extras
- 12 months: pre-existing conditions, joint reconstruction, psychiatric (restricted)

## Who it suits
Members who use everyday extras a lot (regular dental, physio, optical) and want good Silver hospital cover — a strong "switcher" option against other Silver products.
