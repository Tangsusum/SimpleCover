# HealthFirst — Silver Plus Hospital & Extras

> FICTIONAL PRODUCT — for demo only. Not a real health insurance policy.

- **Fund:** HealthFirst
- **Product name:** Silver Plus Hospital & Extras
- **Tier:** Silver
- **Cover type:** Hospital + basic extras
- **Monthly premium (single, base):** $165
- **State availability:** NSW, VIC, QLD, SA
- **Excess:** $500 per admission

## Hospital cover (what's included)
All Silver-tier clinical categories, including:
- Joint reconstruction (e.g. knee, shoulder)
- Cataract surgery
- Hernia and appendix
- Rehabilitation, psychiatric services (restricted)

## Extras cover (annual limits)
- General dental: up to $600/yr
- Optical: up to $200/yr
- Physiotherapy: up to $300/yr

## Not covered
- Pregnancy and birth (Gold-tier item)
- Major dental / orthodontics
- Weight loss surgery

## Waiting periods
- 2 months: general hospital
- 2 months: general extras
- 6 months: major extras
- 12 months: pre-existing conditions, joint reconstruction, psychiatric (restricted)

## Who it suits
Individuals and couples who want solid hospital cover for common procedures plus everyday extras, at a mid-range price.
