# MediSure — Bronze Secure Hospital

> FICTIONAL PRODUCT — for demo only. Not a real health insurance policy.

- **Fund:** MediSure
- **Product name:** Bronze Secure Hospital
- **Tier:** Bronze
- **Cover type:** Hospital only
- **Monthly premium (single, base):** $120
- **State availability:** NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA
- **Excess:** $500 per admission

## Hospital cover (what's included)
All Bronze-tier clinical categories, including:
- Dental surgery
- Hernia and appendix
- Gastrointestinal endoscopy
- Bone, joint and muscle (excludes joint reconstruction/replacement)
- Rehabilitation, psychiatric (restricted)

## Not covered
- Joint reconstruction / joint replacement (Silver+)
- Cataracts (Silver+)
- Pregnancy and birth (Gold)
- Any extras

## Waiting periods
- 2 months: general
- 12 months: pre-existing conditions
- 12 months: psychiatric (restricted)

## Who it suits
People who want affordable hospital-only cover a step above Basic, covering common lower-cost procedures, without paying for extras.
