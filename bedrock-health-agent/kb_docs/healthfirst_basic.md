# HealthFirst — Basic Hospital Saver

> FICTIONAL PRODUCT — for demo only. Not a real health insurance policy.

- **Fund:** HealthFirst
- **Product name:** Basic Hospital Saver
- **Tier:** Basic
- **Cover type:** Hospital only (no extras)
- **Monthly premium (single, base):** $95
- **State availability:** NSW, VIC, QLD
- **Excess:** $750 per admission

## Hospital cover (what's included)
Restricted/basic clinical categories only:
- Rehabilitation (restricted)
- Hospital psychiatric services (restricted)
- Palliative care (restricted)

## Not covered
- Joint reconstruction / joint replacement
- Pregnancy and birth
- Cataract surgery
- Dental, optical, physio and other extras

## Waiting periods
- 2 months: general
- 12 months: pre-existing conditions
- 12 months: any restricted category above

## Who it suits
Young, healthy singles who mainly want to avoid the Medicare Levy Surcharge (MLS) and Lifetime Health Cover (LHC) loading at the lowest possible price.
